11 August 2026Navigation refinement

The sidebar surrendered thirty-eight pixels of territory

I reduced the desktop sidebar from 306 to 268 pixels and the common 390-pixel phone layout from 304 to 270 pixels. Icons, padding, headings, submenu indentation, and platform boxes became more compact while labels remain readable and every navigation target stays at least 44 pixels tall. The seal may now see more of the actual page.

11 August 2026About redesign

The creator introduction developed an actual narrative pulse

I rebuilt About around a personality-first demon-cat dossier, three focused creative beliefs, a playful Creative DNA display, a rotating Current Hauntings shelf, and pinned personal evidence. The repeated card-and-list rhythm is gone, mobile Hauntings stack without cropping, and selective personal details now support the creator story instead of competing with it.

11 August 2026Creator tool

The 66-page worksheet escaped Word and became a cabinet

I rebuilt the site-filling master workbook as 22 phone-first web worksheets covering every public room, both new Hobby spaces, shared maintenance, Project Hubs, and final publishing checks. Each room autosaves privately in the current browser, exports and restores a complete backup, and produces a compact paste-ready submission. I also shortened Hobby Hub navigation, collapsed the repeated demon-cat guide, and reduced oversized mobile hero art.

11 August 2026Hobby Hub expansion

Two new rooms opened and the demon cat gained curatorial powers

I expanded Hobby Hub with The Screening Room, The Illustrated Shelf, Current Fixations, a small Past Fixations archive, compact expandable entries, wax-seal statuses, room inventory counts, and a chooser for completed four-and-five-cat recommendations. Three new demon-cat illustrations supervise the additions, motion remains restrained, and the desktop layout no longer stretches beyond the open sidebar.

11 August 2026Design & navigation

The estate developed a daylight personality

I added a warm blush-cream scrapbook light mode that follows each visitor’s device, plus a temporary theme switch beside Menu. The mobile sidebar became slimmer without sacrificing labels, and Hobby Hub can finally collapse on desktop and remember where its cabinet was left.

10 August 2026Mobile fix

The Closet Door cat stopped sitting on an escape route

I moved the perched demon cat above its own card on mobile so it no longer covers the Scenarios link. It remains dramatic on desktop but has lost its authority to obstruct navigation.

10 August 2026New recommendation

The Playlist received its first three possessions

I added “kiss me,” “oh well,” and “DADDY” with official Spotify links, untouched cover artwork, personal notes, content labels, and players that load only when asked. The aux cord has developed a documented chain of custody.

10 August 2026Visual system

Five demon cats replaced five borrowed emojis

I created a jeweled 1–5 rating mark for Hobby Hub recommendations and made every score readable to screen readers. Recommended Creators remains deliberately rating-free; fellow creators are being showcased, not judged by a tiny feline tribunal.

9 August 2026New site wing

The Hobby Hub opened six highly unnecessary rooms

I published the main Hub, Playlist, Books, Games, YouTube, Inspiration Cabinet, and a permission-only Recommended Creators showcase. Most shelves are still waiting for their first entries, but apparently my interests required real estate.

9 August 2026Mobile fix

The oversized headers were returned to containment

I repaired mobile hero text, page labels, long descriptions, and Coming Soon layouts that were escaping their frames. The words have been reminded that the viewport is not optional.

9 August 2026Website features

Visitor tools, creator resources, and new jewels went live

I published Scenario Finder, Play Help, Update Log, Creator Resources, Requests & Suggestions, custom menu icons, lace accents, and jeweled page objects. Useful first; suspiciously elaborate immediately afterward.

8 August 2026Website feature

I expanded the site beyond one page with Scenarios, Project Hubs, tutorials, a diary, a gallery, responsive navigation, platform filtering, and demon-cat staff. A perfectly restrained response to having a profile.
