Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Project Hub?

It is the one page where I gather a scenario’s premise, characters, platform links, version differences, content guide, model advice, diary notes, and artwork. One useful nest instead of six scavenger hunts.

Why do some projects have two platform buttons?

The scenario is available on both platforms. Each edition may use different memory, limits, opening messages, or updates, and I will explain those differences clearly.

How do content labels work?

Quick labels help you browse. The full Content Guide separates tone, relationships, intimacy, horror, sensitive themes, player role, status, and meaningful exclusions so you can choose safely.

Can I change the player name or pronouns?

Check the project’s Player Role box. It will say whether the role is fixed, customizable, or written for specific pronouns. The box knows; no séance required.

What if a scenario link does not work?

First, check the Update Log for a platform change. If the link is still broken, send me a Website Suggestion through Requests & Suggestions. You did nothing wrong; sometimes links simply choose violence.

Are planned projects playable?

No. The Closet Door holds spoiler-light previews of things I am still building. A real play button appears only after release.
