From inside the locket

I built this because making things is fun.

LesBiChaotic is brand new. At the time of writing, I have exactly ZERO audience—a beautifully round number :p. There is no guaranteed success story hiding behind the curtains. I simply had ideas, became excited, and apparently decided they required an entire jeweled website.

I love watching something that existed only in my head become real on the screen. One idea turns into a page. The page needs decorations. The decorations need custom objects. Suddenly I am making filters, guides, downloadable worksheets, secret lockets, and ten separate menu icons because I have never met a detail I could not become unnecessarily invested in.

I am still learning, experimenting, and discovering what my style even is. That is part of the fun. I get to try something, stare at it, change my mind, make it prettier, and immediately invent three more things. Would success be lovely? Obviously. But joy arrived first—and it already brought luggage. ♡

No audience required. The demon cat still approved the budget.
