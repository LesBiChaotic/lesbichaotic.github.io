I am LesBiChaotic: a small bisexual creator learning in public, obsessing over stories and tiny details, and trying to be brave enough to share what delights me.

Creator introduction

Hello. I brought problems.

I make character-driven scenarios because storytelling makes my brain light up. Expect queer chemistry, sharp tension, horror, multiple love interests, romance with bite, and fictional people who desperately need one honest conversation.

Queer stories matter to me, and I want them to have access to every genre and every kind of ending. Women are usually causing the trouble. Occasionally a man wanders in. Regrettably, he may also be interesting.

House warning

I am learning as I go.

I am shy about sharing, but the process delights me too much to keep everything hidden. I overbuild once I become invested, develop too many ideas at once, procrastinate, and then become frighteningly productive.

I can manufacture heavy angst for narrative seasoning and then cry if a fictional character is mean to me. This system is working exactly as intended.

Creative manifesto

What I want my stories to do.

  • I want characters to feel alive enough to surprise me—and specific enough that their terrible decisions feel painfully inevitable.
  • I want romance to contain real emotional tension. Tender, frightening, ridiculous, or all three before breakfast.
  • I believe ridiculous stories can still matter, and creating should stay playful instead of turning into a performance.
  • I want queer characters to have every genre, every kind of adventure, and every ending—not one carefully approved shelf.
  • Darkness is welcome. I label it honestly so you can choose it; narrative ambush is not cool.
