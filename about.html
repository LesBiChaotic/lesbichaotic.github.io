Creative manifesto

The sweetness has fangs.

  • Let romance be tender, frightening, ridiculous, or all three before breakfast.
  • Give characters enough personality to make their bad decisions feel painfully inevitable.
  • Use clear content labels. The narrative may be unstable; the navigation will not be.
  • Never waste perfectly good yearning by allowing people to communicate efficiently.
